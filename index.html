Who We Are

A church with a heart for God and people

JGSM Church is a Christ-centred family committed to worship, biblical teaching, prayer, compassion, and sharing the hope of Jesus with remote communities.

We believe the local church should be a place where people encounter God, grow in faith, find genuine belonging, and discover their purpose. Our story and detailed church history can be added here later.

Faithful in worship. Fearless in service.
Our Vision

To see lives transformed by the love and truth of Jesus Christ.

Our Mission

Reach the unreached, strengthen families, develop disciples, and serve communities with humility and compassion.
